The Journey

Eight steps to complete financial clarity.

Every step builds on the last. You move only when you're ready — and the tool tracks your numbers and progress throughout.

Stage A — Foundations
Step 1
Budget & Pay Essential Bills
Create a budget, check benefit eligibility, insure essentials, and make minimum payments on all debts. Stabilise before optimising.
❓ Do you rely on credit for essentials?
Stage B — Safety Net
Step 2
Build Your Initial Emergency Fund
Save 1–3 months of essential outgoings in an easy-access account. One unexpected cost should not push you back into debt.
Step 3
Enrol in Your Workplace Pension
Capture the full employer pension match — it's free money added to your salary. Contribute enough to meet the matching threshold.
Stage C — Debt & Buffer
Step 4
Create a Debt Repayment Schedule
List every debt with its APR. Compare against savings rates and prioritise overpaying higher-rate debts using the avalanche method.
❓ Any debt beyond mortgage or student loan?
Step 5
Build Your Full Emergency Fund
With costly debt cleared, expand your buffer to 3–6 months of essentials. Self-employed or variable income? Consider 6–12 months.
Stage D — Goal Planning
Step 6
Define Your Financial Goals
Reassess your budget with newly freed cash flow. Write down each goal with a target amount and classify it as short-term (<5 yrs) or long-term.
Stage E — Long-Term Investing
Step 7
Save for Short-Term Goals
Money needed within 5 years belongs in cash. Explore Cash LISAs, easy-access savings, Premium Bonds, and fixed-rate accounts.
❓ Are short-term goals on track?
Step 8
Long-Term Investing (5+ years)
Choose the most tax-efficient wrapper for your situation — the answer depends on whether you need access before pension age (~58).
❓ Need access before pension age?

Your Personalised Outcome

Every path leads to a clear strategy.

The flowchart ends with a personalised investment recommendation — determined by one key question about when you need your money.

Outcome A
Invest for Long-Term Goals Before Pension Age
For goals you'll access before approximately age 58 — early retirement, large purchases, financial independence. Prioritise flexible, tax-efficient wrappers.
Stocks & Shares ISA
Primary recommendation. Up to £20,000/year. All growth and income is tax-free. Accessible at any time with no penalty.
Stocks & Shares LISA
25% government bonus (max £1,000/year). Only if buying a first home under £450,000. Penalties apply for other withdrawals.
General Investment Account (GIA)
No annual limit. Use once your £20,000 ISA allowance is used up. Capital gains may be taxable.
Key principle: Invest in low-cost, globally diversified index funds. Keep charges as low as possible.
Outcome B
Invest for Retirement & Long-Term Goals
For money you will not need until pension age or later. Pension wrappers offer the most powerful tax advantages available in the UK.
Workplace Pension
Increase contributions beyond the minimum match. Salary sacrifice maximises efficiency — contributions reduce your taxable income directly.
SIPP (Self-Invested Personal Pension)
Especially valuable for 40%+ taxpayers who can claim additional tax relief via Self Assessment. Wide investment choice.
Stocks & Shares LISA
25% government bonus within the £4,000/year limit. Subject to the 25% withdrawal penalty outside qualifying rules.
Key principle: Invest in low-cost, globally diversified index funds using the most tax-efficient wrapper for your circumstances.
